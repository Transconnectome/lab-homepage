## Why Quantum Machine Learning in Connectomics?

The human connectome contains over 86 billion neurons and upwards of 100 trillion synaptic connections. Even at the macroscale of fMRI parcels and diffusion tractography, capturing high-order network interactions and tensor dynamics imposes prohibitive computational and combinatorial barriers on classical supercomputers.

### Research Thrusts in QML
- **Quantum Graph Neural Networks (QGNN)**: Mapping anatomical and functional brain graphs into Hilbert space representations using parameterized quantum circuits.
- **Quantum Kernel Estimation**: Computing high-dimensional similarity kernels between brain states with polynomial quantum speedup.
- **Hybrid Quantum-Classical Optimization**: Seamless integration of PyTorch/Pennylane/Qiskit pipelines running on simulated quantum processors and real cloud QPUs.
