Undergraduate intern Anna Jung gave a talk titled *"Becoming Brainy"* at the AI Institute summer internship Lightning Talk on August 31, looking back on two months in the Connectome Lab. The session brought together 34 internship presentations from research labs and companies. Writing afterwards, Anna Jung said the short format left her thinking about how to carry an audience unfamiliar with a paper from its background to its results within a tight time limit at future conferences.
