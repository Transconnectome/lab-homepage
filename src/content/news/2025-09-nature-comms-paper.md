*"Polygenic architecture of brain structure and function, behaviors, and psychopathologies in children"* — led by Yoonjung Yoonie Joo with lab members Bo-Gyeom Kim, Gakyung Kim, Eunji Lee, Jungwoo Seo, and Prof. Jiook Cha — was published in **Nature Communications**. The study analyzes patterns of covariation among polygenic scores, brain imaging, cognition, behavior, mental health and environmental measures in ABCD children. It connects observations across levels to investigate individual differences in development.
