*"Polygenic architecture of brain structure and function, behaviors, and psychopathologies in children"* — led by Yoonjung Yoonie Joo with lab members Bo-Gyeom Kim, Gakyung Kim, Eunji Lee, Jungwoo Seo, and Prof. Jiook Cha — was published in **Nature Communications**, mapping genome-wide polygenic influences on the developing brain in the ABCD cohort.
