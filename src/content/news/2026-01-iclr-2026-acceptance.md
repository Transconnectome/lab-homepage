*Decoding Dynamic Visual Experience from Calcium Imaging via Cell-Pattern-Aware Pretraining*, a paper by PhD student Sangyoon Bae, has been accepted to the International Conference on Learning Representations (**ICLR 2026**), to be presented in Rio de Janeiro. The work decodes dynamic visual experience from calcium-imaging recordings using cell-pattern-aware pretraining.
