The paper **“Learning brain dynamics across distinct scaling regimes reveals psychiatric signatures,”** led by Sangyoon Bae, was published in *Communications Biology*. The study presents Multi-Band Brain Net (MBBN), which separates resting-state fMRI dynamics into distinct scaling regimes and uses frequency-aware attention to examine predictive performance and network patterns across UK Biobank, ABCD, and ABIDE.
