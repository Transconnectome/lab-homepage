On November 8, 2025, Moon Seokjin, Wang Heehwan, and Oh Kyungjin presented **“Reconstruction of Memory”** at OB/Scene Focus, with research advice from Jiook Cha. The program introduced early experiments using EEG and AI to express emotion and memory through art.
